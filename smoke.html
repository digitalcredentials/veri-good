Verification plan

Click a step to tick it off. Command line first: npm ci && npm test → 28 passed, no second terminal, no browser install. #28

  1. #27Load this page fresh. Press Valid, no expiry, click Verify. Expect: three green checks, card reads Taylor Tuna / Department of Biology. Open Credential details — title, description, date, criteria all filled. Close it.
  2. #27Click Verify Another. Expect: verdict above turns green — all seven rows empty. This is the one step that can fail invisibly: on a broken build the card looks identical and the rows still hold Taylor Tuna.
  3. #31Press Not a credential, click Verify. Expect: message appears beside the paste box, your text is still there, button still says Verify. All seven rows stay empty — nothing reached the card. Previously this cleared your paste and showed three messages.
  4. #31Type a character into the box. Expect: the message disappears as soon as the text changes.
  5. #31Press Tampered, click Verify. Expect: an alert on the card titled “Signature doesn't match”, and no red ring on the box — the paste was fine, the credential wasn't. #error-message fills here.
  6. #30Narrow the browser window to roughly phone width. Expect: the card shrinks with the window and the page never scrolls sideways. Nothing clipped — the paste box and drop zone shrink too.
  7. #30Trigger an error at that narrow width. Expect: the button keeps clear space below it and is never cut off by the card's bottom edge.
  8. #29Turn on VoiceOver (⌘F5) and verify something that fails. Expect: the failure is spoken. Previously it was announced to nobody. The running checks are read out politely as they complete.
  9. #27Press Expired, verify, then Verify Another, then Valid, no expiry and verify again. Expect: every field renders the second time — nothing suppressed or stuck empty after a failure.
