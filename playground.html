What the colours mean. Plain text is the credential's own data — nothing to see. Red is an error the component is reporting, so it is the credential you should be wary of. Red on a tinted row with a left over flag is different and worse: a card field still holding text from the last verification while the card is hidden. That one is a bug in the build, not a problem with the credential, and it is invisible on screen.

All nine are read live from the component's shadow root. They matter because you can't see them: each sits in a wrapper that reset() hides, so text left behind by a previous verification is invisible on screen and identical in a screenshot. This table is the only way to tell.

What to look at

The card shows a verdict. The table above shows what the component actually put in the DOM to produce it — which is not always the same thing, because several of those fields are hidden rather than empty.

Worth trying

  • Verify something, click Verify Another, and watch the table. Anything still filled there is carried over from the last verification and invisible on screen.
  • Compare Expired with Tampered. One is a credential that verified and failed a check; the other could not be trusted at all. Whether the card tells those apart clearly is worth your judgement.
  • Try Not a credential, then check whether your pasted text is still there to fix.
  • Narrow the window to phone width and verify something.
